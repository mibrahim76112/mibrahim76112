## ⭐ LATEST PROJECTS

| 🤖 **Cooperative Safe MADRL** | 🧠 **Imbalance-Aware Transformer for Fault Diagnosis** |
|---|---|
| **[Cooperative Safe MADRL](https://github.com/mibrahim76112/Coperative-Safety-MADRL)** — Hierarchical multi-agent RL for communication-aware UAV coordination. Upper-level policy selects communication links; lower-level handles collision avoidance with Lyapunov-based safety constraints. Reduced violations **2.8× vs CPO/LCPO**, converged **35% faster** with **8× fewer violations**, cut comm-link cost **~50%**. Under review at **AAAI**. | **Imbalance-Aware Transformer** — Transformer combining ArcFace-style cosine-margin learning, class-balanced sampling, and diffusion-based augmentation for **21-class** industrial fault diagnosis on ~250k sensor windows. Achieved **96.3% accuracy / 96.1% macro-F1**, lifting minority-class recall from below 70% to above **90%**, outperforming HGAN, DBN, PRO-SMOTEBoost, and SMOTE+CE. |

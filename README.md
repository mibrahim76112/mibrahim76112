## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm an M.Sc. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Manitoba, working at the intersection of **multi-agent reinforcement learning**, **5G/6G wireless systems**, **UAV autonomy**, **embedded AI**, and **LLM-based agentic systems**.

- 🔭 Researching hierarchical deep RL for communication-aware multi-UAV coordination with Lyapunov-based safety guarantees — paper under review at **AAAI**
- 📡 Working on deep unfolding and SIM/IRS-assisted beamforming for next-generation wireless systems
- 🤖 Building safe cooperative MADRL frameworks for networked autonomous agents
- 🛠️ Background in embedded firmware (ESP32, FreeRTOS), FPGA design, and edge AI deployment (Jetson Nano, TensorRT)
- 🧠 Also building production LLM/agentic AI systems — multi-agent customer support platforms, RAG pipelines, and tool-calling workflows
- 💼 Open to research collaboration and ML/embedded/agentic AI engineering roles
- 📫 Reach me at: **ibrahimxh07@gmail.com**

## ⭐ LATEST PROJECTS

| 🤖 **Cooperative Safe MADRL** | 🧠 **Imbalance-Aware Transformer for Fault Diagnosis** |
|---|---|
| **[Cooperative Safe MADRL](https://github.com/mibrahim76112/Coperative-Safety-MADRL)** — Hierarchical multi-agent RL framework for communication-aware UAV coordination. Upper-level policy selects active communication links; lower-level handles collision avoidance with Lyapunov-based safety constraints. Reduced violations **2.8× vs CPO/LCPO**, converged **35% faster** with **8× fewer violations**, cut comm-link cost **~50%** without losing connectivity. Under review at **AAAI**. | **Imbalance-Aware Transformer** — Transformer combining ArcFace-style cosine-margin learning, class-balanced sampling, and diffusion-based augmentation for **21-class** industrial fault diagnosis on ~250k sensor windows. Achieved **96.3% accuracy / 96.1% macro-F1**, lifting minority-class recall from below 70% to above 90% and outperforming HGAN, DBN, PRO-SMOTEBoost, and SMOTE+CE baselines. |
